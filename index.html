swimming

Friday first

1 — Lom Kojetice

  • Small quarry near Neratovice.
  • Freshwater jellyfish are the identifying detail.
  • Best match for the place you remembered.

2 — Jezero Konětopy

  • Backup option.
  • Parking is inside the recreational area.
  • Clean-water swimming spot.

3 — Jezero Lhota

  • Keep as an easy backup.
  • More infrastructure than Kojetice.
  • Good if convenience matters most.

Weather logic

  • Friday is the warmest day, so it stays the main swimming day.
  • Saturday works better as a flexible city day.
  • Sunday stays deliberately light.
